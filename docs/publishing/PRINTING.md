# Print and duplex proof

The PDFs are US Letter (8.5 × 11 inches), with content inset for ordinary printer margins. Front pages reserve extra blank space at the left (punch/binding edge) and carry the section strip at right. Back pages mirror this: extra space and strip are at right and left respectively, so the strip stays on the outside edge after turning the sheet.

## Required physical proof

1. Print at **Actual size / 100%**, not “fit,” on US Letter paper.
2. Enable background graphics if the print application offers that setting.
3. For a two-page PDF select **two-sided** and **flip on long edge**.
4. Hold the portrait sheet upright, turn it like a book, and confirm the back is upright. A short-edge flip will make the back upside down.
5. Confirm the front strip is at the right and the back strip at the left.
6. Punch a sacrificial copy with the intended three-hole punch. Confirm no text is clipped and the inner clearance is adequate.
7. Review in grayscale: headings, warnings, rules, and status must remain distinguishable without color.

Record printer model, settings, reviewer, and date in the pull request. Printer hardware varies, so CI checks page geometry and overflow but cannot replace this physical test.
