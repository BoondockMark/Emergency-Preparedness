## Before making a report

<ol class="procedure">
<li>Describe your location and where the smoke or fire appears to be.</li>
<li>Note only what you can observe safely: visible smoke or flame, apparent direction, and nearby landmarks.</li>
<li>Do not approach a hazard to collect more details.</li>
</ol>

## Details to have ready

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Your callback information, if requested</li>
<li>Where and when you observed the condition</li>
<li>What is changing, if anything</li>
<li>A safe route away from the area</li>
</ul></div>
