## Enroll every household member

<ol class="procedure">
<li><strong>Open</strong> the verified official enrollment page shown above.</li>
<li><strong>Add</strong> each address where you want location-based warnings.</li>
<li><strong>Choose</strong> every available delivery method you can reliably monitor.</li>
<li><strong>Enable</strong> emergency alerts in the phone's settings and allow alert sounds.</li>
<li><strong>Save</strong> the confirmation, then repeat for another adult's device.</li>
<li><strong>Review</strong> the account after a phone number, email, address, or caregiver changes.</li>
</ol>

## Keep more than one way to receive warnings

<p>Keep a charged phone and backup power. Also identify a verified broadcast or other official source for use during an internet or cellular outage. Do not assume enrollment guarantees delivery; conditions and network failures can delay messages.</p>
