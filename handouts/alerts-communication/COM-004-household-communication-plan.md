<p class="lede">Decide how household members will reconnect, then give each person a copy of this page.</p>

<div class="warning"><strong>Official instructions take priority.</strong> Follow evacuation, shelter, road-closure, and other protective instructions from the responsible public-safety agency. This household plan coordinates your family; it does not replace official alerts or emergency services.</div>

## Make the plan together

<ol class="procedure">
<li><strong>Choose two meeting places:</strong> one just outside the home and one outside the neighborhood. Never travel to a meeting place when officials say the route or area is unsafe.</li>
<li><strong>Choose an out-of-area contact.</strong> Each person should know how to call or text that person. Text may work when voice calls are difficult; do not assume any method will always work.</li>
<li><strong>Plan for access and support.</strong> Include transportation, mobility, medical equipment, medications, power, language, children, pets, livestock, and service animals.</li>
<li><strong>Keep copies.</strong> Put one in each wallet, school bag, go-bag, and vehicle without listing information that would create unnecessary privacy risk.</li>
<li><strong>Practice twice a year.</strong> Update the plan after a move, school or job change, or change in care needs.</li>
</ol>
