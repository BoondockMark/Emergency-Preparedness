<p class="lede">Decide how household members will reconnect, give each person a copy, and review the plan whenever contacts or care needs change.</p>

<div class="warning"><strong>Official instructions take priority.</strong> Follow evacuation, shelter, road-closure, and other protective instructions from the responsible public-safety agency. This plan does not replace official alerts or emergency services.</div>

## Make the plan together

<ol class="procedure">
<li><strong>Choose two meeting places:</strong> one just outside the home and one outside the neighborhood. Never travel there when officials say the route or area is unsafe.</li>
<li><strong>Choose an out-of-area contact.</strong> Each person should know how to call or text that person. Do not assume any one method will always work.</li>
<li><strong>Plan for access and support.</strong> Include transportation, mobility, equipment, medications, power, language, children, animals, and authorized pickup.</li>
<li><strong>Keep copies.</strong> Put one in each wallet, school bag, go-bag, and vehicle without including unnecessary private information.</li>
<li><strong>Practice twice a year.</strong> Update the plan after a move, school or job change, or change in household or care needs.</li>
</ol>

## If household members are separated

<ol class="procedure">
<li><strong>Follow</strong> official instructions for your current location.</li>
<li><strong>Send</strong> one short update with your name, location, safety status, and next action.</li>
<li><strong>Contact</strong> the out-of-area person if household members cannot reach one another.</li>
<li><strong>Conserve</strong> the battery: dim the screen, close unused apps, and retry later if networks are busy.</li>
<li><strong>Update</strong> the contact whenever your location or plan changes.</li>
</ol>
