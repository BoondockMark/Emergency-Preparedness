<p class="lede">Program a radio from a current, authorized channel plan, then verify the saved settings. Use a separate guide for your exact radio model, software, and connection method.</p>

<div class="warning warning--caution"><strong>Confirm compatibility first.</strong> Being able to enter a frequency does not authorize transmission. The radio, operator, and settings must be permitted for the radio service and channel. Ask the radio lead if any requirement is unclear.</div>

## Get the channel plan from the radio lead

| Setting | What to confirm |
|---|---|
| Channel name and memory | Agreed label and destination slot; avoid overwriting a needed channel. |
| Receive frequency (RX) | Frequency your radio listens to. |
| Transmit frequency (TX) | Frequency your radio sends on. Simplex: same RX/TX. Repeater: may differ. |
| Repeater shift or offset | Confirm direction and amount if required; check the resulting TX frequency. |
| Tone or code | Confirm TX and RX separately: none, CTCSS, or DCS; include DCS polarity (normal/inverted) when specified. |
| Mode, bandwidth, power | Exact plan requirements and radio compatibility; use authorized power appropriate to the link. |

<p>CTCSS and DCS filter which received signals open the speaker. They do not encrypt a conversation or make a channel private.</p>

## Load and save the settings

<ol class="procedure">
<li><strong>Identify the exact model and software.</strong> Follow its guide for keypad entry, a cable, or an app. Do not substitute another model's button sequence.</li>
<li><strong>Back up.</strong> Read and save the existing configuration when supported; confirm you connected to the intended radio.</li>
<li><strong>Enter the channel plan.</strong> Set the name, RX, TX, tones/codes, mode, bandwidth, and power. Check decimal places, units, and DCS polarity.</li>
<li><strong>Save to the radio.</strong> Complete the model's write or synchronization step. An entry saved in phone or computer software may not yet be stored in the radio.</li>
<li><strong>Read back the stored channel.</strong> Reopen or read from the radio and compare each value with the plan. Stop if any field is missing or does not match.</li>
</ol>

<p class="lede">A successful write is only the first check. Verify that the radio retains the channel and can communicate as intended.</p>

## Check before returning to service

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Restart as directed; confirm the saved channel remains available.</li>
<li>Select the intended memory/group; check volume and receive audio.</li>
<li>Check the active transmit channel when using dual watch or scanning.</li>
<li>Verify any talk-around or reverse setting against the plan; these can change the transmit path.</li>
<li>When authorized and the channel is clear, make a short radio check with a known station. Follow COM-002 for call procedure.</li>
<li>Confirm both stations can hear and understand each other; a receive-only check does not verify your transmit settings.</li>
<li>Record the plan version, radio, date, and result. Report unresolved problems to the radio lead before duty.</li>
</ul></div>
