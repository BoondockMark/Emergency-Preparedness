<p class="lede">Prepare and practice before an emergency so everyone can leave promptly. Check what is ready; write in what still needs action.</p>

<div class="warning"><strong>Life safety comes first.</strong> If danger is immediate or officials direct you to evacuate, leave now. Do not wait to finish this checklist, gather belongings, or complete home-departure tasks.</div>

## Prepare now—before an evacuation

### 1. Official alerts and terms understood

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>I receive local official alerts in ways I can notice, understand, and act on, including during sleep or a power outage.</li>
<li>I recorded the official alert source(s): __________________________________________________</li>
<li>I know my evacuation zone or other local identifier: ______________________________________</li>
<li>Everyone understands the exact local terms for prepare, leave, shelter, road closure, and re-entry. We know terms vary by jurisdiction and will follow the instructions in each alert.</li>
<li>We will use official information rather than rumors and will not enter a closed area until authorities permit re-entry.</li>
</ul></div>

See **EVS-002** for evacuation levels and terminology.

### 2. Contacts, meeting places, and destinations selected

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Each person carries household and out-of-area contacts on paper and, when possible, on a charged device.</li>
<li>We selected an accessible near-home meeting place and an outside-neighborhood meeting place.</li>
<li>We selected a primary destination and at least one backup that can meet our household's lodging, health, accessibility, and animal needs.</li>
<li>Our plan covers separation at work, school, care, or other regular locations; authorized pickup people are current.</li>
<li>We shared the plan with our contacts and practiced it on: ______________</li>
</ul></div>

Near-home meeting place: ______________________________________________________________<br>
Outside-neighborhood meeting place: ___________________________________________________<br>
Primary destination: ______________________________ Backup: ______________________________

### 3. Two routes and backup transportation planned

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>We mapped two routes from home and routes from school, work, and care locations, while recognizing officials may change routes.</li>
<li>Drivers keep vehicles safely maintained and, when practical, fueled or charged; keys are easy to reach.</li>
<li>Everyone has a ride, safe seating or restraints, and space for essential mobility, medical, child, and animal equipment.</li>
<li>If our usual vehicle, driver, road, or transit service is unavailable, we have a backup transportation plan.</li>
<li>We know how to check official road, transit, and shelter information when leaving.</li>
</ul></div>

Primary route: _________________________________ Alternate route: _________________________<br>
Backup driver/service: __________________________ Phone or contact method: _________________

See **EVS-003** for route and transportation planning.

### 4. Go-bags, medicines, documents, and equipment ready

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Portable go-bags are labeled, reachable, and sized so assigned people can move them safely.</li>
<li>Essential medicines and health supplies are current; refill, storage, refrigeration, and power arrangements have been discussed with the prescriber or pharmacist.</li>
<li>Protected paper or offline copies of identification, contacts, prescriptions, insurance, and essential records are ready.</li>
<li>Phones, alert devices, communication aids, mobility equipment, chargers, batteries, and other necessary equipment are ready to take.</li>
<li>Cash, keys, sturdy footwear, weather layers, water, and ready-to-eat food are positioned with the go-bags.</li>
<li>Supplies are checked on this schedule: __________________ and after every use.</li>
</ul></div>

See **EVS-004** for go-bags and documents and **EVS-005** for medicines, power, and necessary equipment.

### 5. Children, animals, access needs, and neighbors accounted for

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Children know the plan at an age-appropriate level; a responsible adult and backup are assigned to each child.</li>
<li>Animals have identification, carriers or restraints, transport, essential supplies, and accepting destinations.</li>
<li>Each person's mobility, communication, sensory, cognitive, medical, language, and personal-assistance needs have a primary and backup solution.</li>
<li>Service animals remain with their handlers; their transport and supplies are included.</li>
<li>We arranged help with neighbors or support networks without assuming emergency responders can provide individual transportation.</li>
<li>No one will enter or remain in danger to check on another person or retrieve an animal.</li>
</ul></div>

See **EVS-006** for children, animals, access needs, and support networks.

## Only when leaving—and only if time permits

### 6. Safe home-departure tasks

Do these only if officials allow time, conditions are safe, and the task does not delay departure.

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Take the people, animals, go-bags, medicines, necessary equipment, phones, keys, and destination information already prepared.</li>
<li>Wear protective clothing appropriate to current official guidance and conditions.</li>
<li>Close doors and windows and adjust utilities only when specifically directed and only if you already know how to do so safely.</li>
<li>Leave a household contact the planned destination and route without posting sensitive details publicly.</li>
<li>Use the route and transportation officials direct; stay alert for changing instructions.</li>
</ul></div>

<div class="warning warning--caution"><strong>Skip any unsafe task.</strong> Never search for belongings, take a hazardous route, or handle a suspected leak, fire, downed line, or damaged equipment. See <strong>EVS-010</strong> for the detailed home-departure procedure.</div>

## 7. Final “leave now” check

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li><strong>Danger is immediate or officials directed evacuation: leave now—do not delay for this checklist.</strong></li>
<li>Account for people and animals without re-entering danger or waiting for someone who has another safe plan.</li>
<li>Take only the essential items already within reach; do not add last-minute packing.</li>
<li>Follow current official routes and instructions, even when they differ from the household plan.</li>
<li>Go to the safest available destination and monitor official updates when safe to do so.</li>
</ul></div>

<div class="warning"><strong>Once you leave:</strong> do not return until authorities say it is safe. If the planned route or destination is unsafe, follow official directions and use the safest available alternative.</div>
