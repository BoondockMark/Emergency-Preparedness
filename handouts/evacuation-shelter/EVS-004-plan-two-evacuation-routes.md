<p class="lede">Plan and review two ways to leave—but always use the route that officials direct when an evacuation happens.</p>

<div class="warning warning--danger"><strong>DANGER — Current instructions come first.</strong> These planned routes do not override road closures, responder directions, changing fire or hazard conditions, or official evacuation routes. Never enter a closed road or drive toward smoke, fire, floodwater, debris, or another hazard. Leave early when officials direct you to evacuate.</div>
