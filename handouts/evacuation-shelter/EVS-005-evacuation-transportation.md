<p class="lede">Arrange a safe way to leave, a backup if that way fails, and the help each person, animal, and essential device will need.</p>

<div class="warning"><strong>Leave when officials direct you to leave.</strong> Follow current evacuation routes and traffic control. Do not delay departure to finish this worksheet, gather belongings, or wait for a preferred ride.</div>

## If using a vehicle

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li><strong>Maintain it:</strong> follow the manufacturer's routine service schedule; check tires, lights, brakes, fluids, battery, and required safety equipment.</li>
<li><strong>Keep it usable:</strong> maintain enough fuel or battery charge to leave the affected area, accounting for detours and congestion. Know a safe backup fueling or charging option.</li>
<li><strong>Keep access ready:</strong> place keys where authorized drivers can find them, and keep any gate, garage, or charging instructions with the plan.</li>
<li><strong>Load safely:</strong> use a seat and restraint for every person. Secure mobility equipment, carriers, supplies, and other cargo so they cannot move or block the driver's view, controls, or exits. Stay within vehicle and restraint limits.</li>
<li><strong>Carry navigation:</strong> keep current paper maps in case cellular service or navigation fails. Mark more than one route, but use the route officials direct during an evacuation.</li>
<li><strong>Carry emergency supplies:</strong> include water, essential medicines, first aid, phone charging, a flashlight, weather-appropriate clothing, and vehicle emergency items appropriate to local conditions.</li>
<li><strong>Be ready early:</strong> load essential items and position the vehicle before roads become congested—without blocking a road, sidewalk, fire lane, or hydrant.</li>
</ul></div>

## If not driving

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Ask at least two household members, neighbors, friends, or caregivers whether they can provide a ride. Confirm what would cause them to come or call.</li>
<li>Identify regular public transit and paratransit options. Learn eligibility, advance-reservation rules, operating hours, fare, service-area, companion, mobility-device, and animal policies before an emergency.</li>
<li>Learn whether local officials provide evacuation transportation or an assistance registry. Registration may not guarantee a ride; keep another method.</li>
<li>Tell the provider about an accessible pickup location and any boarding help, lift or ramp, securement position, mobility-equipment space, communication support, personal assistant, service animal, pet carrier, or other capacity needed.</li>
<li>Keep the pickup address and entrance precise. Arrange a safe way to reach the pickup point; do not wait outdoors in hazardous conditions.</li>
</ul></div>

## During departure

<ol class="procedure">
<li><strong>Check</strong> current instructions from public officials before moving, if it is safe to do so.</li>
<li><strong>Follow</strong> official routes, signs, checkpoints, lane controls, and directions from emergency personnel. Do not take a familiar shortcut when officials direct another route.</li>
<li><strong>Keep</strong> roads, shoulders, intersections, gates, and access points clear for responders and other evacuees. Stop or park only in a safe, permitted place.</li>
<li><strong>Turn around</strong> rather than drive through floodwater or a closed, damaged, blocked, burning, smoke-obscured, or otherwise unsafe road. Never move a barricade.</li>
<li><strong>Avoid exposing</strong> anyone to smoke, floodwater, fire, downed wires, debris, hazardous materials, or another danger while walking, waiting, loading, transferring, refueling, or charging. Change plans and request help from a safe location.</li>
<li><strong>Tell</strong> the household contact when the group departs and, when safe, when it arrives. Continue monitoring official information because routes and destinations can change.</li>
</ol>

<div class="warning warning--danger"><strong>Do not enter floodwater.</strong> Water can hide a washed-out road and can move a vehicle. Turn around, obey closures, and use an official alternate route.</div>
