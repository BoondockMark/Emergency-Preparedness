<p class="lede">Write a person-directed plan for the assistance, information, equipment, and time you need to leave safely and stay at a destination.</p>

<div class="warning"><strong>Leave before conditions become unsafe when officials tell you to evacuate.</strong> If danger is immediate, use the safest available way to reach safety and tell responders what assistance you want. This worksheet does not replace instructions from emergency officials or individualized advice from your clinician, pharmacist, equipment supplier, or manufacturer.</div>

## Plan for disrupted routines

Plan for <strong>loss of power, separation from your usual support, inaccessible routes, and communication failures</strong>. A diagnosis does not tell another person what assistance you need. Describe what works for you, ask before assistance is given, and revise this plan whenever your needs or circumstances change.

## Keep this plan usable

Review it after every practice, move, change in support, medicine or equipment change, and at the interval you choose. Protect private information; carry only what you need and share it only with people you choose.
