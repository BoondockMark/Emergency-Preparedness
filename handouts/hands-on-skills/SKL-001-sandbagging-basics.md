## Prepare

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Use gloves and suitable footwear</li>
<li>Choose stable ground away from traffic</li>
<li>Keep a clear route away from the work area</li>
</ul></div>

## Sample placement sequence

<ol class="procedure">
<li>Clear loose debris only if it is safe to do so.</li>
<li>Place each bag flat and close gaps between bags.</li>
<li>Stagger joints in later rows rather than lining up seams.</li>
</ol>

## Reassess conditions

<ol class="procedure">
<li>Observe from a safe position.</li>
<li>Stop if water rises or the installation shifts.</li>
<li>Leave before the escape route is threatened.</li>
</ol>
