<p class="lede">Practice moving everyone and essential equipment to a safe ready-to-leave point. This card tests the household plan; it does not replace it.</p>

<div class="warning"><strong>Exercise only—stop for any real alert or danger.</strong> Drills must not imitate an official alert, block a road, create smoke or flames, or require unsafe lifting. Say “DRILL” in every spoken or written drill message. Stop immediately if anyone is distressed, an animal becomes unsafe, equipment could be damaged, or conditions change.</div>

## Use the plan you already made

Review these handouts before scheduling the drill. Correct the plan there rather than copying it onto this card.

* **EVS-001** — readiness, contacts, meeting places, and final leave-now check
* **EVS-002** — go-bags, medicines, documents, power, and essential equipment
* **EVS-003** — local evacuation terms, decision points, and when to leave
* **EVS-004** — two routes, destinations, and a safe place to pause
* **EVS-005** — primary and backup transportation, pickup, loading, and access
* **EVS-006** — children, caregivers, animals, handlers, and loading practice

## Before-drill checklist

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>The six EVS handouts above are current, and every participant knows this is a future exercise even though the start time will be unannounced.</li>
<li>A responsible organizer chose a calm practice window, checked weather and surroundings, and told neighbors or building staff if the activity might concern them.</li>
<li>The organizer selected an unmistakable household-only cue containing the word <strong>“DRILL”</strong>; it does not copy an alert tone, siren, sender name, message format, evacuation order, or emergency-service wording.</li>
<li>Every participant may pause or opt out. Children and people who could be distressed know what “DRILL” means; no surprise cue will be used for anyone who needs advance notice.</li>
<li>The stop point is on private property or another permitted safe area <strong>before any sidewalk crossing, public road, traffic lane, or vehicle movement into traffic</strong>.</li>
<li>Exits and accessible paths are clear. The drill uses no smoke, flame, damaged route, live hazard, road obstruction, unsafe transfer, or lift beyond a person's training and ability.</li>
<li>Phones remain available for real calls. The observer will cancel the drill for a real alert, emergency, injury, distress, unsafe animal behavior, or blocked safe exit.</li>
</ul></div>

## Run the drill

<div class="warning warning--caution"><strong>Unannounced simulated alert:</strong> At an unannounced time within the agreed practice window, the organizer says or sends: <strong>“DRILL—household evacuation practice. Use our plan now.”</strong> Do not broadcast it publicly or use emergency tones, sirens, agency names, or official-alert styling.</div>

<ol class="procedure">
<li><strong>Start and notify.</strong> Timekeeper records the start. The lead gives the household-only cue. Everyone states or demonstrates their assigned action. <strong>Start time:</strong> __________</li>
<li><strong>Gather people.</strong> Caregivers move household members by the practiced accessible path to the planned stop point. Account for everyone; do not search beyond the drill area. <strong>People gathered:</strong> __________</li>
<li><strong>Gather essential equipment.</strong> Assigned people bring only staged go-bags, keys, medicines, communication and mobility aids, and animal equipment identified in EVS-002 and EVS-006. Do not add last-minute packing. <strong>Equipment gathered:</strong> __________</li>
<li><strong>Practice transport contact or loading.</strong> Make only a prearranged practice call or text and begin safe loading in a legal, off-road location. Say “DRILL” to the helper. Do not summon emergency services or an unbriefed provider. <strong>Contact / loading complete:</strong> __________</li>
<li><strong>Stop before public traffic.</strong> Stop at the named safe point before entering or crossing a public road, moving a vehicle into traffic, or beginning the planned route. Account for every person, animal, and essential item. <strong>Ready-to-leave time:</strong> __________</li>
</ol>

## Choose the variants your household needs

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li><strong>Children:</strong> use simple words and a familiar adult; practice shoes, comfort or communication items, hand-holding, car seats, and the meeting point. Never hide or pretend a caregiver is missing.</li>
<li><strong>Animals:</strong> use the usual handler and species-appropriate carrier, leash, harness, or restraint. Practice calmly and stop for fear, aggression, escape risk, or unsafe lifting. Use EVS-006 for large-animal practice; do not improvise loading.</li>
<li><strong>Mobility devices:</strong> use the normal accessible path and trained assistance. Include device controls, charger or power, transfer aids, and securement notes. Never carry a person or device on stairs or perform a transfer unless the people involved are trained and it is routinely safe.</li>
<li><strong>Medication:</strong> retrieve the prepared, labeled supply or an empty practice container from its normal safe location. Maintain required security and temperature; do not delay or change a dose for the drill.</li>
<li><strong>Transportation assistance:</strong> practice the agreed call or message with a pre-briefed helper, including pickup entrance, passenger count, mobility-device space, animal capacity, and boarding support. Clearly say no actual pickup is requested unless one was scheduled.</li>
</ul></div>
