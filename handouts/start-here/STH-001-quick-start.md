<p class="lede">Do these first. Write down your choices, share them with your household, and practice them.</p>

<div class="warning"><strong>If danger is immediate:</strong> follow official instructions and move to safety. Do not delay an evacuation to finish a plan or collect supplies.</div>

## Today: make contact possible

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Choose one out-of-area contact everyone can reach.</li>
<li>Save local emergency-alert and trusted information sources.</li>
<li>Pick two meeting places: one near home and one outside your neighborhood.</li>
<li>Write the plan on paper; place a copy in each go-bag.</li>
</ul></div>

**Go deeper:** Use **STH-003 — Know Your Local Hazards** to choose local alerts and **STH-005 — Child & Caregiver Planning** to make contacts, reunification plans, and responsibilities work for children and anyone who depends on care.

## Next: plan how you will leave

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Choose more than one route and decide where you could stay.</li>
<li>Plan transportation now; include a backup if you do not drive.</li>
<li>List the help, equipment, medication, communication, and power each person needs.</li>
<li>Include every animal: carrier or restraint, transport, identification, supplies, and a destination that will accept it.</li>
</ul></div>

**Go deeper:** Use **STH-004 — Disability & Access Planning** for individual access and functional needs, and **STH-006 — Animal Planning** for pets, service animals, and other animals.

## Then: know what may happen

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Ask your local emergency-management authority which hazards affect your address and what actions it recommends.</li>
<li>Use **STH-003 — Know Your Local Hazards** to record the official alerts and protective actions for your location.</li>
<li>Practice one part of the plan, then update names, routes, and needs.</li>
</ul></div>

<p><strong>Build supplies next:</strong> use <strong>STH-002 — Build a Two-Week Preparedness Kit</strong>. Store what your household can safely carry and use.</p>

<p><strong>Keep the plan usable:</strong> use <strong>STH-007 — Annual Binder & Kit Review</strong> every year and after a household change, move, drill, or emergency. Replace expired supplies and update contacts, routes, equipment, medications, and care needs when they change.</p>
