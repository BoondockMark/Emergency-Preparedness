<p class="lede">Pack a small go-bag first. Then build home and vehicle supplies around your household's needs.</p>

<div class="warning warning--caution"><strong>Keep supplies usable:</strong> store them where household members can reach them. Check dates, batteries, seasonal needs, and fit on a regular schedule.</div>

<div class="grid">
<div>

## Water, food, and light

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Water for drinking and sanitation</li>
<li>Nonperishable food everyone can safely eat</li>
<li>Manual can opener and basic utensils</li>
<li>Flashlights or headlamps</li>
<li>Spare batteries or safe charging options</li>
</ul></div>

## Information and protection

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Battery-powered or hand-crank radio</li>
<li>Paper contact list, plan, and local map</li>
<li>Copies of key records in a protected container</li>
<li>First-aid supplies and dust masks</li>
<li>Whistle, work gloves, and sturdy shoes</li>
<li>Cash in small bills</li>
</ul></div>

</div>
<div>

## Personal and access needs

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Medications and a current medication list</li>
<li>Glasses, hearing-aid supplies, and communication tools</li>
<li>Mobility-device supplies and backup power plan</li>
<li>Personal-care, hygiene, and climate items</li>
<li>Comfort and sensory-support items</li>
</ul></div>

## Animals

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Food, water, medicines, and bowls</li>
<li>Carrier, leash, harness, or other safe restraint</li>
<li>Identification, photo, and care records</li>
<li>Sanitation items and familiar bedding</li>
</ul></div>

</div>
</div>

## Pack for the way you may leave

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Label bags; keep essential items light enough for the intended person to carry.</li>
<li>Put needed medical, communication, mobility, and animal items where they can leave with you.</li>
<li>Add address-specific items only after checking current guidance from your local emergency-management authority.</li>
</ul></div>
