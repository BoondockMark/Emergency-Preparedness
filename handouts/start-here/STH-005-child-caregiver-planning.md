<p class="lede">Make one reunification and care plan for children and anyone who relies on another person for transportation, communication, personal care, supervision, or decision support.</p>

<div class="warning"><strong>Official instructions take priority.</strong> Follow evacuation, shelter, school-release, and other directions from the responsible authority. Never return to a closed or dangerous area to reach a meeting place.</div>

<li>Each caregiver understands the school or care site's emergency, release, and reunification rules.</li>
<li>Children and other household members know who may pick them up and how to reach the out-of-area contact.</li>
<li>Transportation can carry each person, essential equipment, supplies, and service animal together.</li>
<li>Caregivers have keys, permissions, identification, instructions, and safe access to needed supplies.</li>
<li>A paper copy is stored in each go-bag and given to each caregiver in a usable format.</li>
</ul></div>
