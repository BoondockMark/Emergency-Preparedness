<p class="lede">Use this log to keep home, vehicle, and go-bag supplies current, safe, and ready to carry.</p>

<div class="warning warning--caution"><strong>Follow product instructions:</strong> replace expired, damaged, leaking, recalled, or improperly stored items. Store medicines, food, water, batteries, fuel, and chemicals as their labels and qualified guidance direct.</div>

## Review checklist

<div class="checklist"><ul>
<li>Water and food are intact, within date, and suitable for household needs.</li>
<li>Medicines and medical supplies were reviewed using professional and label guidance.</li>
<li>Lights, radio, chargers, and batteries work; damaged batteries were removed safely.</li>
<li>Clothing, masks, footwear, hygiene items, and animal supplies still fit current needs.</li>
<li>Contact lists, plans, maps, identification copies, and cash are current.</li>
<li>Each intended carrier can safely lift or move the assigned bag.</li>
</ul></div>
